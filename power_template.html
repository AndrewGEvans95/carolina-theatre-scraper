READING THE SIGNAL

A little context.
A clearer picture.

Full means sold. The meter shows tickets purchased as a share of total capacity. Held seats are set aside by the theatre; they are neither sold nor available.

A dash means unknown. Sales changes need an earlier snapshot at least 6 or 24 hours old. They can span longer gaps between checks and include returns.
